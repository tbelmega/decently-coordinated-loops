# Outbox

The mirror of [INBOX.md](INBOX.md): everything agents need FROM {{OWNER}} —
questions, proposals, approval requests — aggregated in one place, answerable in
one line each. Both queues trend toward empty: agents drain the inbox, {{OWNER}}
drains the outbox.

**For {{OWNER}}:** answer any entry by writing on its `> A:` line — locally or from
your phone, any time, in any order. Partial answers and "later" are valid answers.
Then tell any agent "process the outbox".

**For agents:** the entry contract, answer routing, and interview mode live in the
loops-queues skill (`{{DCL_HOME}}/skills/loops-queues/SKILL.md`).
